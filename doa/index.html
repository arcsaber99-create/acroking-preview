DOA policy

Every order is packed with care and sent by air freight for the best coral condition. If a coral does arrive dead, this is how we put it right.

What is covered

Our dead on arrival (DOA) cover applies to air freight orders delivered within New Zealand. A coral counts as DOA when it has no living tissue when you first open the bag.

Partial tissue loss, colour change, or a coral that has not opened up yet is not a DOA. Send us photos anyway and we will review it case by case.

How to make a claim

  1. Be there for delivery. Someone needs to receive the box and open it promptly.
  2. Take clear photos. Photograph the coral in its unopened shipping bag, then out of the bag, with its condition easy to see.
  3. Contact us within 2 hours. The window starts at the delivery time recorded by the freight carrier. Email hello@acroking.example with your name, the affected coral, and your photos.
  4. Keep everything. Hold on to the coral and the packaging until we have reviewed your claim.

Claims sent without photos, or after the reporting window, may be declined.

If your claim is approved

We will offer a replacement where we can, or store credit for the value of the affected coral, to use on livestock. WYSIWYG pieces are one of a kind, so an identical replacement is not always possible.

Credit does not include shipping. We do not give refunds on livestock or shipping charges.

What is not covered

  • Orders where no one was available to receive the delivery.
  • Orders sent to an incorrect or incomplete address supplied at checkout.
  • Late arrival caused by weather, flight, or freight carrier delays outside our control.
  • Pickup orders once you have collected and inspected them.

If something goes wrong outside this policy, please still get in touch. We will always do what we can to help.
